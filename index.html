          FAQ

          Questions from the trench.

          What is Trench County?

          Trench County is a Roblox open-world game built around memecoin trading, inspired by pump.fun. Players launch their own coins and trade them against each other on RBXfun, the trading platform on every PC in town, then spend what they make.

          Is it free to play?

          Yes. Trench County is free on Roblox. Hit Play on the game page and you're in — straight from the Roblox app.

          Do I need a crypto wallet?

          No. Everything happens inside the game. There's nothing to connect and nothing to install beyond Roblox.

          How do I launch a coin?

          Sit at any PC at Trench Cafe (or press T on foot), open the terminal and hit Launch. Choose a name, a ticker and an image, link one of your posts, and your coin is live for every server. You earn 0.5% of every trade other players make on it.

          What does “graduating” mean?

          Every coin starts on a bonding curve: buys push the price up, sells push it down. When enough people buy in to fill the curve, the coin graduates (at 85 ROL) — the Trench County version of making it.

          What is ROL?

          ROL (Rolana) is the town's version of Solana. Its price follows the real SOL price, kept between $90 and $150. Swap $ and ROL on your phone or a PC's Wallet, or top up with Robux: 5 ROL for 150 R$.

          What can I spend it on?

          Real cars at VELLANO, five houses on the hill, chains and briefcases at AUREUM, suits at Greys, guns at Liberty Arms, repairs and resprays at Halloran Garage. MONARCH casino plays with free daily chips, never with money.

          Is there a token?

          One is coming to Solana. The contract address goes up on this site first. In-game coins, ROL and $ are simulated and have no real-world value.
