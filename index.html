          FAQ

          Questions from the trench.

          What is RBXfun?

          RBXfun is a Roblox experience built around memecoin trading, inspired by pump.fun. Players launch their own coins and trade them against each other inside the Executive Internet Cafe.

          Is it free to play?

          Yes. RBXfun is free on Roblox. Hit Play on the game page and you're in — straight from the Roblox app.

          Do I need a crypto wallet?

          No. Everything happens inside the game. There's nothing to connect and nothing to install beyond Roblox.

          How do I launch a coin?

          Sit at any PC in the cafe, open the terminal and hit Launch. Choose a name, a ticker and an icon, and your coin is live for the whole server.

          What does “graduating” mean?

          Every coin starts on a bonding curve: buys push the price up, sells push it down. When enough people buy in to fill the curve, the coin graduates — the RBXfun version of making it.

          Is the game finished?

          Not yet. RBXfun is in BETA, so expect new features, balance changes and the odd wipe. Favourite the game on Roblox to hear about updates.
