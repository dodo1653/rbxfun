The story

Your story starts at the counter.

I

You just got here with $250.

Not even gas money.

Everyone spawns at Trench Cafe: brick, green paint, a radio on the back counter, eight PCs by the window. Nobody here was born rich.

II

So first, you get a j*b.

$25 a go. Nobody's proud of it.

Clock in behind the counter: grind, pull the shot, steam the milk, carry it to table two. Or take the green council cart and sweep Main Street, $25 a bag.

III

Swap your $ for ROL. Sit at any PC.

Every coin on Pulse, the second it launches.

ROL is the town's Solana. Its price follows the real SOL price, so the market outside moves the market in here. Swap on your phone, then take a seat.

IV

Buy early. Sell higher.

Or launch your own and take a cut of every trade.

Coins run on a pump.fun bonding curve and graduate at 85 ROL. Launch one in a click, link your post, bullpost it in Trench Chat, and earn 0.5% of every buy and sell anyone makes.

Print enough, and life changes.

And once you've made it, have fun with your money.

      FAQ

      Asked at the counter.

      What is Trench County?

      A free Roblox open-world game built around memecoin trading, inspired by pump.fun. You trade coins other players launch, earn money, and spend it in town. RBXfun is the trading platform inside it.

      Do I need a crypto wallet?

      No. Everything happens inside Roblox. There's nothing to connect.

      Is ROL real crypto?

      No. ROL is the town's simulated Solana. Its dollar price follows the real SOL price, kept between $90 and $150, so the outside market moves the game.

      Can I buy ROL?

      Yes, with Robux: 5 ROL for 150 R$, or the same Robux for that ROL's worth in cash. Tap the + on your Cash or Rolana card, or Add funds in the PC's Wallet.

      How do I launch a coin?

      Sit at any PC (or press T on foot), open the TC Terminal and hit Launch. Pick a name, a ticker and a head or an image, and link one of your posts. You earn 0.5% of every trade other players make on it.

      Is the casino real money?

      No. MONARCH plays with chips you collect free once a day at the cashier. Chips can't be bought, and they never turn back into $ or ROL.

      What happens when I die?

      70% of the cash in your wallet drops where you fell, for anyone to pick up. Money in the bank and in your briefcase is safe.
